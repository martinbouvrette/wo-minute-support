# Privacy Policy — Wo Minute

*Last updated: November 10, 2026*

## In one sentence

Wo Minute collects, stores and transmits **no personal data**. None. Period.

## In detail

**No account.** The app asks for no name, no email, no sign-up.

**No collection.** Wo Minute contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracker of any kind. It talks to no server.

**Everything stays on your device.** Your settings (lengths, chosen paintings, colors, sounds) are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. If you delete the app, they go with it.

**Notifications.** If you allow them, Wo Minute schedules local notifications to tell you when a timer ends, even with the screen off. They are generated by your device and never leave it.

**Voices.** The app's voices — the end-of-timer announcement, the book reading itself, the names of the balls, the robot — use your device's built-in speech synthesis. Nothing is recorded, nothing is sent.

**Children.** Since the app collects no data, children can use it with no risk to their privacy.

## Questions

For any question about this policy: support@wominute.com
